Wald 95\% CIs use the normal quantile, since statsmodels provides no Satterthwaite or Kenward-Roger degrees of freedom. The estimand is conditional on the trained artifacts, with seeds indexing artifacts and the patient as the sampling unit; these intervals do not rest on the patient alone. The model has no arm-by-patient term and one residual variance for all arms of a model, reference rows included. In a contrast of two arms with the same seeds, the patient, window and seed effects cancel. Its interval then rests on that pooled episode-level residual variance and does not carry the between-patient spread of the contrast that the paired $t$ interval carries. In a contrast of an arm with seeds against an arm without seeds or with fewer seeds, the seed variance component enters the interval, which then treats the seeds as sampled. Contrasts between the two mask sources are not modeled, since each model holds one mask source. The planted lateral-bias rows apply the contrasts of the REINFORCE control to the final translation error. The control's rule reads the lateral displacement, which no model here holds, so these rows need not agree with Table~\ref{tab:checks-summary}.
